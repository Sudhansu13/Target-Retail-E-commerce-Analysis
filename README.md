# Target Retail – SQL Business & Sales Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Target's Brazilian e-commerce sales data using SQL to understand customer behavior, order trends, sales performance, freight costs, and delivery efficiency.

The analysis covers customer distribution, order growth, monthly seasonality, order timing, state-wise sales, freight charges, and delivery performance. The findings are translated into business insights and recommendations.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze the overall structure and characteristics of the Target dataset
- Identify trends in the number of orders over time
- Understand monthly seasonality in order volume
- Identify the time of day when customers place most orders
- Analyze e-commerce activity across Brazilian states
- Compare customer distribution across states
- Evaluate order value and freight costs by state
- Analyze actual delivery time against estimated delivery dates
- Identify states with high and low freight and delivery times
- Generate actionable business recommendations

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### 1. Exploratory Data Analysis

The analysis begins by examining the structure of the customer and order tables, including column data types, order date ranges, and customer geographic coverage.

The dataset covers **4,119 cities and 27 states**.

### 2. Order Trend & Seasonality Analysis

The analysis evaluates yearly and monthly order trends.

Orders increased significantly from **329 in 2016 to 45,101 in 2017 and 54,011 in 2018**.

August recorded the highest monthly order volume, while September recorded the lowest.

### 3. Customer Behavior Analysis

Customer orders were categorized into Dawn, Morning, Afternoon, and Night.

**Afternoon** recorded the highest order volume with **38,135 orders**, followed by Night and Morning.

### 4. State-wise Customer & Sales Analysis

The analysis compares customer distribution and order values across Brazilian states.

São Paulo (SP) had the highest number of customers at **41,746**, followed by RJ and MG.

SP also recorded the highest total order value, while BA had the highest average order price.

### 5. Freight & Delivery Analysis

Freight costs were analyzed across states to identify areas with higher logistics costs.

The analysis identified **RR, RO, PB, PI, and AC** among the states with the highest average freight values.

Delivery performance was also evaluated by comparing actual delivery time with estimated delivery dates. The analysis highlighted significant differences in delivery performance across states.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Focus promotions and faster delivery initiatives in high-volume states such as SP.
- Investigate low-order months and use targeted campaigns to improve demand.
- Optimize logistics in states with high average freight costs.
- Consider regional warehouses or local courier partnerships in high-cost delivery regions.
- Monitor delayed deliveries and improve estimated delivery dates.
- Use state-wise customer and sales behavior to support targeted marketing and product recommendations.
